How to Board the Bay Shuttle

Instructions on how to board the ship🚢

Kansai International Airport

Ticket Counter / Bus Stop

  • Please get on the connecting bus for KANSAI International Airport terminal(pier).
    ※The Port Terminal Pier at Kansai Airport is a no-go zone for vehicles, including taxis, and on foot.

▼ Terminal 1

Please purchase tickets at Kobe-Kansai Airport Bay Shuttle ticket counter and then board the shuttle bus
for the pier at bus stop No. 12. After arriving at Kansai Airport pier, please board the Bay Shuttle.
(The Bay Shuttle takes about 30 minutes to Kobe Airport.)

  • Ticket Counter
  • Bus Stop No.12 bound for pier

▼ Terminal 2

Please purchase tickets at Kobe-Kansai Airport Bay Shuttle ticket counter and then board the shuttle bus for the pier at bus stop . After arriving at Kansai Airport pier, please board the Bay Shuttle.

Kobe Airport

Ticket Counter / Bus Stop

  • 2〜3min. by bus or 5〜6 min. on foot.
  • Bus available from the terminal to pier (included in the passenger fare).
  • KOBE Airport Kaijyo Access Terminal
  • Ticket Counter
  • pier
